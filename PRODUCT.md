# Product

## Register

product

## Users

HKUST students browsing courses on a phone between classes or while planning a semester. They need to find a course quickly and understand its description and prerequisite trail.

## Product Purpose

Explore the supplied Clear Water Bay course catalogue offline. Success means a student can select a semester and department, find a course by code or title, open its details, and navigate prerequisites without mistaking extracted links for formal AND/OR logic.

## Brand Personality

Familiar, confident, and student-friendly. Use the visual language of the user-supplied USThing screenshots closely, especially the blue navigation surfaces and simple course rows.

## Anti-references

- Do not copy unrelated USThing modules, grade statistics, timetable data, or section information into this course explorer.
- Avoid generic dashboard decoration, dense nested cards, and decorative icons that obscure course information.

## Design Principles

1. Put finding a course first: search and filters precede the result list.
2. Keep course identity visible: code, title, semester, and department must be easy to scan.
3. Preserve the source prerequisite wording alongside any extracted links.
4. Make every next action obvious with familiar navigation and touch targets.
5. Stay simple enough that the implementation can be explained in an interview.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use readable contrast, screen-reader labels for interactive controls, touch targets around 44 points or larger, and text that wraps on small phones. No meaning depends on color alone. Avoid decorative animation; respect reduced-motion preferences for any future motion.
